Background

Rumah Anak Yatim Tambunan is a caring welfare home located in the interior district of Tambunan, Sabah. The home is associated with the important mission of giving children a safer place to grow, learn, and participate in community life.

For many children, consistent care can make a meaningful difference. A supportive home environment helps provide meals, basic needs, school preparation, emotional encouragement, and opportunities to develop positive values.

Day to day, the home is run by house parents and caretakers who live alongside the children, supported by a small team of staff handling administration, schooling coordination, and welfare matters. The home depends almost entirely on donations from the public, mosques, companies, and visiting well-wishers to keep its doors open.

Core Values

  • Care: Treat children with respect and patience.
  • Education: Encourage learning and school attendance.
  • Community: Build support through volunteers and donors.
  • Hope: Help children feel confident about tomorrow.

Purpose of the Home

The main purpose of an orphanage or welfare home is to provide shelter and daily care for children who require protection, guidance, and support. In a close community such as Tambunan, public awareness and responsible assistance can help strengthen this purpose.

"Every child deserves kindness, safety, and the chance to keep learning."

Visitors who want to help should always communicate politely with the organization, ask what is currently needed, and respect the privacy and dignity of the children.

Brief History

Rumah Anak Yatim Tambunan is the only welfare institution of its kind administered under the Sabah Islamic Religious Affairs Department (JHEAINS) since 1997, having previously been managed by the Sabah Islamic Religious Council. Its founding was inspired by former Chief Minister of Sabah, Y.A.B. Dato Harris Mohd. Salleh.

Construction began in 1981 and was completed in November 1983 on 15 acres of land at a cost of RM5.3 million. The home began operating that same month with its first 54 residents from across Sabah, and was officially opened by Malaysia's then Deputy Prime Minister, Y.A.B. Dato Musa Hitam, on 3 August 1984.

Functions

  • Manage the admission of orphans in accordance with Islamic requirements.
  • Maintain the complex and propose improvements to JHEAINS.
  • Provide adequate comfort, welfare, and education for the children.
  • Act as secretariat to JHEAINS and the RAY Management Board.

Philosophy

The home prioritizes academic excellence and good character grounded in Islamic education, emphasizing both physical and spiritual development, in order to nurture devout, knowledgeable, practicing, and well-mannered Muslims in line with the demands of religion, race, and nation.

Objectives & Goals

  • Help orphans enjoy a fulfilling life within the community, in line with Islamic teachings.
  • Provide a comfortable and complete place to live.
  • Educate and guide children through both formal and informal learning, for worldly and spiritual benefit.
  • Raise independent, high-character, knowledgeable, and devout individuals.
  • Reflect Islam's concern for the welfare of orphans in need.

Who Can Apply

Admission is intended for orphaned children who meet the following key requirements:

  • Children who have lost their mother and/or father.
  • Aged 7 to 12 at the time of application (care continues up to age 19).
  • Muslim (non-Muslim children may be considered with the guardian's written consent for an Islamic upbringing).
  • Applications must include certified parents' death certificates, a certified birth certificate copy, and certified identification of the parent or guardian.
  • Applications must be supported by figures such as the village Imam or headman, the District Welfare Officer, a JHEAINS liaison officer, a medical officer, or a school headmaster/supervisor.

Application forms are available at Rumah Anak Yatim Tambunan or at JHEAINS District Liaison Offices, and should be submitted to: Pengetua, Rumah Anak Yatim, Peti Surat 36, 89657 Tambunan.
